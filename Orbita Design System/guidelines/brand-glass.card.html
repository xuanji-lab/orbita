--glass-bg
One glass background per theme — every layer paints that same value.
Depth is not authored, it is composited. A surface at alpha a nested n deep reads as 1-(1-a)ⁿ:
.60 → .84 → .936light
.70 → .91 → .973dark
Layer 1 is the app boundary, layer 2 the content pane, layer 3 the input box and cards. Change --glass-alpha and the whole ladder re-spaces itself.
The two alphas are deliberately unequal: dark blocks more (.70) than light (.60). Light text needs a settled ground, so letting the wallpaper bleed through costs more in dark than it does in light.
